Terms

account
Your place on an exchange server: your login, your password and your devices. This app only signs in: accounts are created in Fast Media Sorter.
broadcast
A live stream that a person finds and plays, from another device of the account or from a link.
broadcasting
What the sender does: the phone's microphone or camera, or the watch's microphone, goes out as a live stream.
cast to your own device
Sending a broadcast to another device of yours. That device asks first, unless you turned on playing without asking.
catalog
The main screen: your list of channels.
channel
One radio station, video stream or camera in your list.
Channel tile
A tile on your home screen that opens one channel.
connection string
A line of text from the host of an exchange server that holds its address and name and can hold your login. The app fills in the sign-in form from it.
exchange server
A program that you run yourself, or that a person you know runs for you. It connects your own devices when they cannot reach each other directly, so a broadcast from one device can be found and played on another.
playlist
A list of channels in a file or at an address, in M3U format. Importing it adds them to the catalog.
relay through the server
The exchange server passes a broadcast on to its listeners; the content is encrypted on your device first.
via Google Drive
Your devices announce their broadcasts through your own Google account, with no server and no typed address.
