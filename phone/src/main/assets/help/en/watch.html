StreamsPlayer on the watch

What this is for

Play your channels on the watch and broadcast its microphone. The watch takes its channels from your phone. This page opens on the phone, because the watch screen is too small to read it.

In this order

  1. Open Channels 2 and tap a channel. Audio opens the audio player and video opens the video player 3.
  2. To keep audio playing after you leave the app, switch on Keep playing in background in the audio player 3. The row Playing in background 1 then appears at the top of the start screen and brings you back to the player.
  3. To broadcast the watch microphone to listeners on the same Wi-Fi, open Broadcast 4 and press Start broadcast. Show QR code 5 displays the code that the listening device scans.
  4. Choose tile channel 6 sets the channel that the StreamsPlayer tile opens.
  5. Open Channel pictures 7 to remove the downloaded pictures or to download them again.
  6. Press Help 8 at any time to open this page on your phone.

The elements

1Playing in backgroundReturns to the player of the channel that keeps playing.

The row is on the start screen only while a channel is playing in the background. Tapping it opens the audio player of that channel.

2ChannelsThe channels on your watch.

The list comes from the published catalog, plus the channels your phone sends. Search streams, Filter streams and Sort streams narrow it, and Refresh catalog downloads the catalog again. Tapping a channel starts it. Its picture is the preview for a video channel, otherwise the station's logo, otherwise its small icon. The watch downloads these channel pictures itself when the list first loads and after Refresh catalog, except the sets you removed under Channel pictures.

The Own filter shows the live broadcasts of your other devices first, grouped by device, and then your saved streams. Under Own, Refresh loads the broadcasts again without downloading the catalog. The watch plays audio broadcasts; Save keeps one as a channel. Broadcasts appear here when the watch is connected to your exchange server.

If the watch is offline or the connection is too slow for the stream, the player says so.

3PlayerPlays the channel you chose.

Both players have Play or Pause, Jump to live, Pin stream or Unpin stream, Open on phone and Go back. The audio player also has Previous, Next, Keep playing in background, Volume down, Volume up and Dim screen.

Open on phone shows the channel on your phone.

Undo
Unpin stream takes a pinned channel back off the list of pinned channels.
4BroadcastBroadcasts the watch microphone.

Start broadcast opens the microphone and sends it to listeners on the same Wi-Fi. While it runs the screen says On air; Screen off darkens the screen without stopping it. Without the microphone permission the screen offers Allow microphone.

When the watch is connected to your account, every broadcast is also announced on your exchange server from its start, and the screen says whether it is on the server; without an account the screen says the broadcast is reachable on this network only. To connect the watch, open Exchange server on the phone and choose Connect watch. Help on the phone opens the page about the watch on your account.

Undo
Stop ends the broadcast.
5QR codeShows a code for the listening device.

While the watch is broadcasting, Show QR code displays a QR code. Scan it on the listening device to listen. When no broadcast is running, the screen says there is nothing to scan.

6Choose tile channelChooses the channel that the tile opens.

Tap a channel in the list. The StreamsPlayer tile on the watch then opens that channel.

Undo
Choose another channel to change it.
7Channel picturesRemoves the downloaded channel pictures or downloads them again.

The screen lists the two sets of pictures, Channel previews and Station logos. Each shows whether the watch holds it and, once the published size is known, how large it is. Download fetches a set. Remove asks first and then deletes the set from the watch; the watch does not fetch it again, not even after Refresh catalog, until you press Download here.

What you remove on the watch does not change the phone, and the pictures on the phone do not change the watch.

Undo
Download fetches a removed set again.
8HelpOpens this page on your phone.

The page opens in the language of the phone. When the phone is not in front, it shows a notification: tap it. The watch tells you if the phone did not answer or if notifications are off for StreamsPlayer on the phone.
