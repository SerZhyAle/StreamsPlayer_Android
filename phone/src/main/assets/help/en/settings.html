Help index/Settings

Settings

What this is for

Everything you can set in StreamsPlayer, on three pages: General, Broadcast and About. A change applies at once and is kept. There is nothing to save, and the app does not restart.

In this order

These settings have no fixed order. They are grouped below by page.

The elements

The top bar

1HelpOpens this help.

On the Broadcast page it opens the page about the broadcast settings. F1 on a hardware keyboard does the same.

Pages

2List of pagesMoves between General, Broadcast and About.

On a wide screen the list stays beside the page. On a narrow one it comes first: pick a page, and the back arrow returns to the list. The screen opens on the page you left last, at the place you left it.

General

3ThemeLight, dark, or as the system is.

As in the system follows the device's dark theme, also when it changes while the app is open. The change applies at once on every open screen.

Undo
Pick another theme.
4LanguageThe language of the app.

Each language is listed under its own name. As in the system follows the language of the device. The change applies at once on every open screen, without a restart.

Undo
Pick another language.
5Enable live broadcastingAllows live broadcasting.

Allows live audio broadcasting and shows the shortcuts for it. While it is off, the Broadcast page says so and offers the way back here.

Undo
Turn it off again.
6Updating the channel listHow the channel list stays current.

Suggest when I open checks, each time you open the channel list, whether a newer catalog is published, and offers it in a bar above the list. Only when I ask makes no check: the list changes only through Refresh catalog in the menu. Automatically on Wi-Fi downloads a newer catalog by itself, on Wi-Fi and at most once a day. The check downloads no file and sends nothing about you.

Undo
Pick another position.

Channel pictures

7Channel previewsThe preview pictures of video channels.

Shows the state of the set - not downloaded, downloading with its percent, downloaded, a newer set ready, or failed - and its size. Download fetches it in the background with a progress notification, Update replaces the copy you have, and Try again repeats a failed download. Remove deletes the set from this phone after you confirm, and the channel list stops showing it the next time you are on the list. Your channels are not touched. After a catalog update the app offers a removed set again.

Undo
Press Download to fetch the set again.
8Station logosThe logos of stations and channels.

Works like the previews above, for the logos. A channel without a preview shows its logo, and one with neither keeps its small icon.

Undo
Press Download to fetch the set again.

About

9VersionThe version you are running.

Taken from the installed package, so it is the one to quote when you report a problem.

10LicenseOpens the license in your browser.

StreamsPlayer is free software under the MIT license.

11Privacy policyOpens the privacy policy in your browser.

What the app stores and sends and where, in your language where the policy has it.

12Documentation and helpOpens the list of help pages.

The pages about each screen of the app. They are part of the app and need no network.

13Official portalOpens the guides in your browser.

The official pages about the media exchange and live broadcasts.

14Support and issue trackerOpens the issue tracker in your browser.

Where to report a problem or read about known ones.

15Source code repositoryOpens the source code in your browser.

The public repository of this app.

16Download and updatesOpens the page this edition is updated from.

The store listing for the store edition and the releases page for the other one. The page opens only when you press this; the About page itself contacts nothing.

17Export diagnostic reportShares a diagnostic report.

Makes a report about the device and playback, without private data, and hands it to the share screen. It goes only where you send it.

18Related productsThe other programs of the family.

FastMediaSorter sorts and organizes media, and StreamsPlayer for Windows is this player on a desktop. Each name opens its site in your browser.

19Components and librariesThe main components the app is built on.

The full list with licenses is in the notices file inside the package.
