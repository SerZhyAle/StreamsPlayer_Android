## Method and its limit

Two web searches on 2026-11-13 (standard mode), read as the persona's own situation: "an internet radio app for a phone
and a watch" and "an Android app that plays a stream address or a network camera". The sources are store pages,
comparison listicles and a vendor guide, not a corpus of reviews or forum threads. The ticket asks for real reviews and
threads (P1); this pass settles the **shape** of the alternatives and the forbidden-name list, and leaves the persona's
verbatim wording as a refinement for the first checkpoint after the listing exists (P10), when real reviews of the
alternatives can be read in the owner's browser. Nothing published depends on that refinement.

## What the persona does today, by situation
