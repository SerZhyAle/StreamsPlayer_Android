# Changelog

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Package Versioning](docs/contracts/PACKAGE-VERSIONING.md).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- With the watch connected to an exchange account, every watch broadcast is announced on the server from its start, with no switch, and the connection to the server stays open while the broadcast runs, whichever screen is in front. The watch's Broadcast screen says whether the broadcast is being announced, is on the server or has failed, with **Why won't it connect?**; without an account it says the broadcast is reachable on this network only. The **Relay through the server** switch of the watch is gone.
- With an exchange server added, every broadcast of the phone is announced on it from its start, sound and camera alike, with no switch to turn on; a server added while the phone is on air takes the broadcast at once, and removing it keeps broadcasts on your own network. Broadcast Control names the server and says whether the broadcast is being announced, is on the server, runs the relay through the server, or stays on your network; without a server it says the broadcast is reachable on this network only and opens the exchange server screen on a tap. The two **Publish through your exchange server** rows of the broadcast settings and the publish switch of the exchange server screen are gone; the server card says what the server's operator sees and links the portal page about going on air.
- Broadcast Control opens the broadcast settings with a **Broadcast settings** button; they are now the Broadcast page of Settings, with one row each for audio and for the camera where a choice is remembered per family.
- The exchange server screen folds into three headed parts, **Via Google Drive**, **Exchange server** and **Live broadcasts**, which stay the way you leave them; its commands keep their own width and wrap, and **Sign out on this device** stands last, apart from the others.
- Check boxes on the exchange screen and in Filter & Sort use the shared option row: the box and the name toggle it, the explanation does not. Commands of the import dialog, the QR scanner, the broadcast list and Filter & Sort keep their own width and wrap.
- The exchange screens of the phone and the watch use the family's words in English, Russian and Ukrainian: exchange server, relay through the server, via Google Drive, cast to your own device, broadcast and broadcasting. Refusals and failures read as the shared message table does, the broadcast list shows a broadcast's mode in words, and the app names the programs of the family by their names.
- The exchange server screen, the broadcast settings and the watch's Broadcast screen link the portal page that explains that screen, in the app language (English where the portal has no page in it); the watch opens it on the phone with **Help on the phone**.
- The exchange glyphs of the shared icon set mark connecting a device (**Connect watch**), the relay through the server and the guest account.

### Added
- The phone offers a newly published channel list: when you open the list, it asks GitHub whether a newer catalog is published - a short request that downloads no file - and only then shows **Update the channel list?** above the list, with **Update** and **Close**. On a fresh install the bar offers the first list. **Update** runs the same import as **Refresh catalog**, the channel pictures offers included; **Close** keeps that list from being offered again, and a list published later is. **Settings > General > Updating the channel list** chooses **Suggest when I open** (the default), **Only when I ask**, which makes no check at all, or **Automatically on Wi-Fi**, which downloads a newer catalog without asking, at most once a day. The privacy policy and the Data safety answers name the check.
- The help index lists every screen and control in a subject index, each linking the pages that describe it, and explains the words the app uses (broadcast, exchange server, channel and others) in a short list of terms, in English, Russian and Ukrainian.
- Channel pictures: the list and the grid show a video channel's preview or the station's logo, ahead of the small icon. After a catalog update the phone offers to download the previews and the logos, each with its size. The watch downloads the same pictures itself when its channel list first loads and after **Refresh catalog**; nothing is sent between the phone and the watch for it. Newer pictures are noticed from the published manifest, and when it cannot be read what you have stays.
- Channel pictures can be removed and fetched again in **Settings > General**, under the **Channel pictures** heading: one row for the channel previews and one for the station logos, each with its state (not downloaded, downloading with its percent, downloaded, a newer set ready, or failed), its size and the buttons that fit - **Download**, **Update**, **Try again** and **Remove**. **Remove** asks first, names the set and what it frees, and the channel list stops showing those pictures the next time you are on it. A download from Settings is the same background download as accepting the offer after a catalog update, with the same progress notification.
- The watch has a **Channel pictures** row on its start screen, between **Choose tile channel** and **Help**. It opens a screen with the two sets - channel previews and station logos - each with whether the watch holds it, its published size once the manifest answers, and **Download** or **Remove**. **Remove** asks first and deletes the set from the watch; the watch does not fetch it again, **Refresh catalog** included, until you press **Download** there. What you remove on the watch does not change the phone.
- A **Settings** screen, opened from the catalog's menu: a list of pages beside the page on a wide screen, and the list first on a narrow one. **General** holds the theme (as in the system, light or dark) and the language, both applied at once without a restart, and the live broadcasting switch. **Broadcast** holds the broadcast settings. **About** holds the description, the running version, the license and privacy pages, help, the official portal, support, the source repository, the download and update page of this edition, the diagnostic report and the related products. The screen reopens on the page and at the place you left.
- The exchange server has one picture across the family, its shared mark: a five-coloured, slightly tilted star. It stands beside the server's name on the exchange screen's sign-in heading and account card, before an error that comes from the server, in the server certificate dialogs, on the broadcast screen while a broadcast is published through the server, beside the Exchange server topic of **Media exchange and broadcasts**, and on the status line of **Live from my devices** on the phone and the watch when the server is missing, needs a new sign-in or cannot be reached. The watch shows it on the alert that explains why the relay through the server failed. The mark is the same in the light and the dark theme and is not mirrored in right-to-left languages.
- Help on the watch: a **Help** row on the watch's start screen opens the page about the watch app on the phone, in the phone's language (English, Russian or Ukrainian; English for the other languages). The page explains the watch's channels, audio and video players, broadcast, QR code and tile screens. When the phone is not in front it shows a notification to tap, and the watch says if the phone did not answer or its notifications are off.
- In-app help for the catalog, Filter & Sort, Add channel, the video player, Broadcast Control and its settings, the QR scanner and the channel tile setup: a **?** action at the end of each screen's top bar, and **F1** with a hardware keyboard, open a page in the app that shows the screen with numbered markers, the usual order of use and every element explained, with links to the neighbouring screens. Channel actions, Recently played, the sleep timer, Import playlist and Import live broadcast carry the same **?** in their title row; it opens the catalog page at the entry that explains them. The pages are bundled in the package, work without a network and are written in English, Russian and Ukrainian; the other interface languages show the English pages.
- In-app help for the exchange server screen and its connection check: the **?** in the top bar and **F1** open the exchange server page, which opens with the server's mark and explains signing in, the account and the live broadcasts; the **Connection check** dialog carries the same **?** in its title row and opens that page at the entry that explains the check. The Broadcast Control page shows the mark beside its title.
- Privacy policy for the first store release in English, Russian and Ukrainian, hosted as the pages under `site/`: what the app stores and sends and where, the exchange server, what the server can and cannot see, Google Drive, the watch, accounts and how to delete them, and every permission with its purpose. **Privacy policy** in Settings > About opens it in the app language (English for the other languages). The permissions table is generated from the merged store release manifests and checked; the Play Data safety answers are written down flow by flow in `docs/play/PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md`.
- Broadcast announcements via Google Drive: sign in with your own Google account on the Exchange screen to list the broadcasts your other devices announce, and turn on **Announce via Google Drive** (off by default, per mode) to announce this phone's broadcasts in the hidden app folder of your Drive. App-folder permission only; the stream never goes through Drive. EN, RU and UK strings; privacy policy section 7.
- Scan QR codes to fill the exchange sign-in form or import a live broadcast, with local frame processing and paste/file alternatives.
- Watch exchange enrollment through the phone, independent encrypted credentials, audio relay through the server, screen-bound presence, descriptor playback and coordinated account removal; shared JVM exchange core.
- Independent StreamsPlayer Android codebase extracted from FastMediaSorter.
- Three application modules: Phone (`phone`), Wear OS companion (`wear`), Watch Face (`watchface`).
- Streams catalog with streaming radio, HLS/DASH/RTSP live video playback.
- Live broadcast producer mode: phone microphone and camera, wear microphone broadcast.
- Distribution variants: `store` and `noLegal` sideload.
- Mechanical version stamping adhering to PACKAGE-VERSIONING rules.
- Release pipeline and pre-release sweep verification tools.

### Fixed
- Searching the channel list with the full catalog loaded shows the matching channels at once, instead of the whole list for about a minute first; a search with no match no longer shows old channels beside "No channels found". A search, or clearing it, now starts the list from the top.
- The widget channel picker follows a search over the full catalog as it is typed, without the rows of the earlier keystrokes; a search, or clearing it, starts the list from the top.
